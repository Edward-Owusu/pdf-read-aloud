How it works

  • 1. Choose a PDFReading begins straight away and continues to the last page.
  • 2. Follow alongThe sentence being read is highlighted and the page scrolls with it.
  • 3. Take controlClick any sentence to start there. Space bar pauses and plays.
  • 4. Come back laterIt remembers where you stopped. Scanned PDFs work too.

Voices come from your browser and device. Microsoft Edge and Chrome usually offer the most natural voices. On phones, keep the screen on while it reads.

Nothing is uploaded: your PDF is opened and read entirely inside your browser. Built by Edward Owusu, CISA.
