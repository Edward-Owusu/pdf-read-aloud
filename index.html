PDF Read Aloud

Upload a PDF and it reads the whole document to you, from start to finish, at a normal speaking pace. Sit back and listen.

How it works

  • Choose a PDF. It begins reading aloud straight away and keeps going to the last page.
  • The sentence being read is highlighted, and the page follows along.
  • Click any sentence to start reading from there. Press the space bar to pause or play.
  • Close the tab and come back later: it remembers where you stopped.
  • Scanned PDFs work too. Text is recognized page by page, which takes a few seconds per page.

Voices come from your browser and device. Microsoft Edge and Chrome usually offer the most natural voices. On phones, keep the screen on while it reads.

Nothing is uploaded: your PDF is opened and read entirely inside your browser. Built by Edward Owusu, CISA.
